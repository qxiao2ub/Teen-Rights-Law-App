# Community Code of Conduct

This project is intended to support respectful, safe legal education for teenagers.

Participants must:

- Treat others with respect.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or humiliation.
- Never post names, contact details, addresses, passwords, private images, or identifying case information.
- Never request help with violence, hacking, doxxing, blackmail, evidence destruction, or evading authorities.
- Clearly label personal opinions and avoid presenting them as verified legal advice.
- Report safety concerns to responsible adults or authorities rather than trying to manage emergencies in a public thread.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that creates safety, privacy, or legal risk.
