# Legal Content and Youth-Safety Review Checklist

Use this checklist before adding or releasing a topic module.

## Legal accuracy

- Identify the jurisdiction and effective date.
- Link each specific legal statement to an official source.
- Separate statutes, regulations, court decisions, school policy, platform policy, and general safety guidance.
- Have a qualified reviewer approve the plain-language summary.
- Add a review-expiration date and content owner.
- Test for misleading omissions and overgeneralization.

## Teen readability

- Use short sentences and define necessary legal terms.
- Avoid fear-based language.
- Include a realistic example and a safe next step.
- Explain what the app does not know.
- Test comprehension with teenagers and educators using an approved research process.

## Privacy and safety

- Do not request names, contact information, exact addresses, account passwords, health information, immigration status, or detailed incident narratives unless a separately reviewed feature truly requires it.
- Provide a non-public escalation path for abuse, exploitation, threats, and emergencies.
- Never ask users to upload intimate images.
- Block public sharing of identifying information.
- Keep moderation rules human-reviewable and appealable.

## AI governance

- Version the knowledge base and model configuration.
- Record the source and reviewer for every topic.
- Evaluate false reassurance, unsafe escalation, and jurisdiction errors.
- Keep reinforcement learning away from legal rules and safety policy.
- Require human approval before deploying model or content updates.
